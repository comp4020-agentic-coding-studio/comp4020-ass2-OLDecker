# Why structure?

SLOP1795 · week 1

---

## A sequence is not a shape

`MVLSPADKTNVKAAWGKVGAHAGEYGAEALERMFLSFPTTKTYFPHF...`

That's the first line of a real protein. It tells you almost nothing about
what the molecule looks like, or does.

# Shape is function

---

## Three examples

- an ion channel is a shape with a hole exactly the right size for one kind of ion
- an antibody's binding site is a shape that matches its target the way a key matches a lock
- an enzyme's active site holds its substrate in the one geometry that lets the reaction happen

None of this is visible in the sequence alone.

---

## Where structures come from

The **Protein Data Bank** (PDB) — one shared, public archive of experimentally
determined structures, going back to 1971. Every structure you'll touch this
semester either comes from it or gets compared against something that does.
