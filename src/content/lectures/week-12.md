Structure-based drug design pulls together nearly everything covered this
semester — a solved or predicted structure, a characterised binding pocket, a
docked candidate ligand, and a judgement about how much to trust each step —
into a single applied pipeline. This closing lecture uses it as a lens back
over the semester, then looks ahead to where this material continues: applied
structural biology, computational method development, or just being someone
who can read a structure critically in whatever field comes next.

## Outline

- structure-based drug design as the semester's ideas in one pipeline
- a recap, week by week, of what fed into what
- where this goes next, inside and outside a structural biology career
- final projects, presented this week
