"These look similar" needs a number behind it before it's useful. This
lecture covers structural alignment (superposing two structures in 3D
regardless of sequence) and RMSD as its standard output, then what happens
once you can compare folds at scale: classification schemes that group
proteins by shared architecture even when their sequences have drifted past
recognition.

## Outline

- structural alignment vs sequence alignment — different inputs, different
  blind spots
- RMSD: what it measures, and why a low RMSD over a short region can hide a
  large disagreement elsewhere
- fold classification, in outline — shared architecture across unrelated
  sequences
- this week's session: aligning two structures yourself and reading the
  output
