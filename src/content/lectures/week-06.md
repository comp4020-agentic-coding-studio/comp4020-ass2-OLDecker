Not every sequence has a solved structure. This lecture covers the classical
answer: find a related sequence that does, align the two, and use the known
structure as a template — homology modelling — plus what an alignment's
conservation pattern tells you about which residues actually matter to
function, independent of whether you model anything at all.

## Outline

- pairwise and multiple sequence alignment, in outline
- reading conservation: a residue conserved across distant relatives is
  probably load-bearing
- homology modelling: template choice, and where the method breaks down
