## Before the session

Have your final project in a presentable state — it doesn't need to be
submitted yet, just showable.

## In the session

Five minutes each: your structure, the question you asked about it, and what
you found. A couple of minutes of questions follow each presentation — this
is often where a gap in the analysis surfaces before submission, not after.

## Afterwards

The [final project](/assessments/final-project/) is due at the end of this
week. That's the last piece of graded work in SLOP1795.
