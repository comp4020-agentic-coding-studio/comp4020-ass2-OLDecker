## Before the session

Nothing to prepare. A ligand-bound structure is assigned to you.

## In the session

Show the bound ligand distinct from the protein, `select` everything within a
set distance of it, and go through those residues to find at least one
concrete interaction holding the ligand in place — a hydrogen bond partner, a
hydrophobic pocket wall, a charged residue nearby.

## Afterwards

If your final project structure has a ligand, binding site or interface, this
is the technique you'll reuse to characterise it in
[week 11's consultation](/sessions/11-project-consultation/).
