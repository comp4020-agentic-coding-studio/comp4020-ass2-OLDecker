## Before the session

Come with a PDB ID in mind, and a one-sentence question you want your final
project to answer about it.

## In the session

One-minute pitches: your structure, your question, why it's the right size
for the time left in semester. Feedback is aimed at scope — is this too big,
too small, or missing an obvious complication.

## Afterwards

Your choice isn't contractually final, but changing it later costs you time
you don't get back. [Week 11's consultation](/sessions/11-project-consultation/)
assumes you're working on the structure you pitch here.
