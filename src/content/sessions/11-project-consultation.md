## Before the session

Bring whatever you have — a partial script, a figure, a paragraph of
findings, or a list of what hasn't worked yet.

## In the session

A short one-on-one slot per person. The goal is a concrete next step, not a
progress report for its own sake — if everything is going fine, say so and
use the time to get ahead instead.

## Afterwards

The final project is due at the end of week 12. This is the last scheduled
checkpoint before then.
