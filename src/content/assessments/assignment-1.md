## The brief

> Choose one PDB structure and write the report you wish existed the first
> time you opened it.

Pick any structure that interests you — not necessarily the one from week 1.
Read its header, work out how it was solved and how much to trust it, and
describe its fold well enough that someone who has never seen it could follow
your figures. Then say something about what the shape means: a binding
pocket's geometry, an interface holding two chains together, a loop that's
missing because it moves too much to resolve. The report is judged on whether
your reading is *accurate*, not on how impressive the structure is.

## What you submit

A PDF, roughly 800–1200 words, with at least two PyMOL renders you made
yourself (a full-structure cartoon and one close-up of the region you
discuss). Cite the PDB entry by its ID. If you used an AI tool for any part of
drafting, analysis or rendering, say so and say what it did — the course's
integrity policy on the [policies](/policies/) page covers what counts as
fine and what needs declaring.
